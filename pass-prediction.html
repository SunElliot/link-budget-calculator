📖 How to use

  1. Paste a current TLE (both lines) from CelesTrak — the fresher, the more accurate.从 CelesTrak 粘贴最新的两行根数 TLE(两行都要)——越新越准。
  2. Enter your ground-station latitude, longitude and altitude, or click 📍 Detect to use your location.输入地面站纬度、经度、海拔,或点 📍 Detect 使用当前位置。
  3. Set the minimum elevation (horizon mask) and how many hours ahead to search.设置最低仰角(地平遮挡)和向后预测的小时数。
  4. Click Predict passes to list upcoming passes with AOS, LOS, maximum elevation and duration.点 Predict passes 列出未来过境的 AOS、LOS、最大仰角和时长。
  5. Times are shown in your browser's local timezone; everything runs on your device.时间按浏览器本地时区显示;全部在本地运算,坐标不上传。
  6. Click 🔗 Share to share the TLE + station setup as a link.点 🔗 Share 把 TLE + 地面站设置作为链接分享。

How satellite pass prediction works

A TLE (Two-Line Element set) encodes a satellite's orbit at a reference epoch. This tool runs the standard SGP4 propagator (via the open-source satellite.js library) to compute the satellite's position over time, then converts it to look angles (azimuth/elevation) from your ground station. Whenever elevation rises above your horizon mask, a pass begins (AOS — Acquisition of Signal) and ends when it drops below again (LOS — Loss of Signal).

For accurate predictions, always use a fresh TLE (less than a few days old) — orbital elements decay quickly, especially for low CubeSats. Times are shown in your browser's local timezone. Everything runs locally; your coordinates never leave your device.
